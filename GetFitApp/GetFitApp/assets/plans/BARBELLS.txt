========================================
TRAINING PLANS: BARBELLS
========================================

----------------------------------------
GOAL: LOSE WEIGHT
----------------------------------------

Commitment: 1 Month
**ONE MONTH? STOP PLAYING.**

Commitment: 3 Months
Focus: Barbell Complexes & Metabolic Conditioning (Light to moderate weight, minimal rest).
* Monday: Full Body Complex (4 rounds unbroken: 8 Barbell Rows, 8 Cleans, 8 Front Squats, 8 Push Press, 8 Good Mornings)
* Tuesday: Upper Body & Core (Barbell Floor Press 4x15, Pendlay Rows 4x15, Barbell Rollouts 3x15)
* Wednesday: Lower Body Burn (Barbell Back Squats 4x15, Barbell Romanian Deadlifts [RDLs] 4x15, Barbell Calf Raises 4x20)
* Thursday: Barbell HIIT (10 rounds: 30 sec max Barbell Thrusters, 30 sec rest)
* Friday: Deadlift & Carry (Moderate Deadlifts 5x10, Barbell Zercher Carries 4x45 sec)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 6 Months
Focus: Density Training (AMRAPs - As Many Rounds As Possible - to maximize calorie burn).
* Monday: 15-Minute AMRAP (10 Barbell Deadlifts, 10 Barbell Hang Cleans, 10 Barbell Push Presses)
* Tuesday: Heavy/Light Contrast (Heavy Barbell Bench Press 4x8, superset with Empty Barbell Push-ups max reps)
* Wednesday: 15-Minute AMRAP (10 Barbell Front Squats, 10 Barbell Reverse Lunges, 10 Barbell Rollouts)
* Thursday: Upper Body Burnout (Barbell Strict Press 4x12, Barbell Bicep Curls 4x15, Barbell Skull Crushers 4x15)
* Friday: The "Bear Complex" (5 rounds of 7 unbroken sets: Power Clean, Front Squat, Push Press, Back Squat, Push Press - light weight)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 9 Months
Focus: Heavy Lifting + Explosive Metcons (Preserving muscle mass while incinerating fat).
* Monday: Heavy Lower (Barbell Back Squats 5x5, Heavy Barbell Hip Thrusts 4x8)
* Tuesday: Fast Metcon (21-15-9 reps for time: Barbell Thrusters, Barbell Facing Burpees)
* Wednesday: Heavy Upper (Barbell Bench Press 5x5, Barbell Pendlay Rows 5x5)
* Thursday: Fast Metcon (10-to-1 descending ladder: Power Cleans, Barbell Jump Squats)
* Friday: Full Body Density (Barbell Push Press 5x8, Barbell Front Squats 5x8, minimal rest)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 12 Months
Focus: The Ultimate Shred (Alternating EMOMs and High-Volume Complexes).
* Monday: 20-Min EMOM (Even minute: 10 Barbell Back Squats; Odd minute: 10 Barbell Push Presses)
* Tuesday: Pull & Core (Barbell Snatch-Grip Deadlifts 4x10, Barbell Rows 4x12, Barbell Suitcase Holds 4x30s/side)
* Wednesday: 20-Min EMOM (Even minute: 8 Barbell Power Cleans; Odd minute: 12 Barbell Reverse Lunges)
* Thursday: Upper Volume (Barbell Floor Press 5x15, Barbell Upright Rows 5x15, Barbell Curls 5x15)
* Friday: "Grace" Protocol (30 Clean & Jerks for time - light/moderate weight to keep heart rate maxed)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

----------------------------------------
GOAL: BUILD ENDURANCE
----------------------------------------

Commitment: 1 Month
**ONE MONTH? STOP PLAYING.**

Commitment: 3 Months
Focus: Muscular Stamina (Empty bar or very light weight, 15-25 rep ranges).
* Monday: Lower Body Stamina (Empty Bar Squats 3x25, Empty Bar Good Mornings 3x20)
* Tuesday: Upper Body Stamina (Empty Bar Overhead Press 3x20, Empty Bar Rows 3x25)
* Wednesday: Core & Carry (Barbell Overhead Walk 4x60 sec, Barbell Rollouts 3x20)
* Thursday: Full Body Flow (Empty Bar Thrusters 4x20)
* Friday: Posterior Chain Stamina (Light Barbell RDLs 3x25, Light Barbell Hip Thrusts 3x25)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 6 Months
Focus: Time Under Tension (Timed sets).
* Monday: 40s Work / 20s Rest Circuit (4 rounds: Barbell Squats, Barbell Push Press, Barbell Rows)
* Tuesday: Grip Endurance (Barbell Shrugs max reps in 60s x 4 sets, Barbell Holds for time)
* Wednesday: 40s Work / 20s Rest Circuit (4 rounds: Barbell Lunges, Barbell RDLs, Barbell Floor Press)
* Thursday: Core Endurance (Barbell Landmine Rotations 4x60 sec, Barbell Rollouts 4x45 sec)
* Friday: The Empty Bar Marathon (10 minutes continuous movement without putting the bar down: mix of curls, presses, squats)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 9 Months
Focus: Volume Accumulation (High total rep targets).
* Monday: The 100-Rep Challenge (100 Barbell Back Squats, break as needed)
* Tuesday: The 100-Rep Challenge (100 Barbell Push Presses, break as needed)
* Wednesday: The 100-Rep Challenge (100 Barbell Deadlifts, break as needed, light weight)
* Thursday: The 100-Rep Challenge (100 Barbell Bent-Over Rows, break as needed)
* Friday: Full Body Accumulation (50 Thrusters, 50 Power Cleans, 50 Front Squats)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 12 Months
Focus: Elite Barbell Stamina.
* Monday: 20-Minute Continuous Complex (Row to Hang Clean to Front Squat to Press - do not drop the bar)
* Tuesday: 10x10 German Volume Training (Barbell Bench Press 10x10, Barbell Rows 10x10)
* Wednesday: 10x10 Lower Body (Barbell Back Squat 10x10, Barbell RDL 10x10)
* Thursday: Iron Cardio (30 minutes: 1 Barbell Clean, 1 Barbell Squat, 1 Barbell Press every 30 seconds)
* Friday: The "Isabel" & "Grace" Combo (30 Snatches for time, rest 5 mins, 30 Clean & Jerks for time - light weight)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

----------------------------------------
GOAL: GAIN SPEED
----------------------------------------

Commitment: 1 Month
**ONE MONTH? STOP PLAYING.**

Commitment: 3 Months
Focus: Explosive Fundamentals (Learning to move the bar with maximum velocity).
* Monday: Power Lower (Barbell Jump Squats 5x5, Light Barbell Speed Squats 4x8)
* Tuesday: Power Upper (Explosive Barbell Push Press 5x5, Barbell Speed Bench 4x8)
* Wednesday: Hip Extension (Barbell Kettlebell-style Swings [holding one end of bar] 4x10, Broad Jumps over empty bar 4x8)
* Thursday: Olympic Intro (Barbell Hang High Pulls 5x5, Barbell Hang Cleans 5x5)
* Friday: Multi-Planar Speed (Barbell Landmine Rotations 4x8/side explosively, Barbell Thrusters 4x6)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 6 Months
Focus: Dynamic Effort & Rate of Force Development.
* Monday: Dynamic Effort Lower (Speed Box Squats 8x3 at 50% max, focusing on bar speed)
* Tuesday: Dynamic Effort Upper (Speed Bench Press 8x3 at 50% max, Explosive Barbell Rows 4x8)
* Wednesday: Triple Extension (Power Cleans from the floor 5x4, Barbell Jump Lunges 4x6/leg)
* Thursday: Dynamic Effort Deadlift (Speed Deadlifts 8x2 at 50% max, resetting each rep)
* Friday: Contrast Training (Heavy Back Squat 1x3, immediately followed by 5 unweighted explosive jump squats - 4 rounds)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 9 Months
Focus: Advanced Olympic Lifting Variations.
* Monday: Power Snatch Mastery (Hang Power Snatch 5x3, Overhead Squats 4x5)
* Tuesday: Upper Ballistics (Push Jerk 5x3, Barbell Drop-and-Catch Rows 4x6)
* Wednesday: Speed Strength Lower (Front Squats with 3-second pause at bottom, explode up - 5x4)
* Thursday: Power Clean Mastery (Power Clean + Push Jerk 5x2+2)
* Friday: Velocity Circuits (6 rounds for time: 3 Power Snatches, 5 Barbell Jump Squats)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 12 Months
Focus: Elite Power Peaking.
* Monday: Complex Training Lower (Heavy Deadlift 1x2 -> 3 Broad Jumps -> 4 Barbell Jump Squats - 4 sets)
* Tuesday: Complex Training Upper (Heavy Bench 1x2 -> Explosive Push-ups -> Speed Barbell Rows - 4 sets)
* Wednesday: Core Power (Heavy Barbell Russian Twists/Landmines 5x6/side, Barbell Rollouts 4x10)
* Thursday: Max Power Testing (Work up to heavy double in Power Clean or Power Snatch)
* Friday: Peak Velocity (Barbell Speed Squats using accommodating resistance [if bands/chains available, otherwise 40% bar weight moving at absolute max speed] 10x2)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

----------------------------------------
GOAL: GAIN STRENGTH
----------------------------------------

Commitment: 1 Month
**ONE MONTH? STOP PLAYING.**

Commitment: 3 Months
Focus: Linear Progression (The classic 5x5 model to build a raw strength foundation).
* Monday: Heavy Lower (Barbell Back Squat 5x5, Barbell RDL 3x8)
* Tuesday: Heavy Push (Barbell Bench Press 5x5, Barbell Overhead Press 3x8)
* Wednesday: Heavy Pull (Barbell Deadlift 1x5 [heavy], Barbell Pendlay Row 5x5)
* Thursday: Hypertrophy Legs (Barbell Front Squat 3x8, Barbell Lunges 3x8/leg)
* Friday: Hypertrophy Upper (Barbell Incline Press 3x8, Barbell Curls 3x10, Barbell Skull Crushers 3x10)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 6 Months
Focus: Periodization & Heavy Triples.
* Monday: Lower Strength (Barbell Back Squat 5x3 [heavier than 3-month plan], Barbell Good Mornings 4x6)
* Tuesday: Push Strength (Barbell Bench Press 5x3, Heavy Barbell Push Press 4x5)
* Wednesday: Pull Strength (Barbell Deficit Deadlifts 4x4, Barbell Bent-Over Row 5x5)
* Thursday: Accessory Strength Lower (Barbell Pause Squats [2 sec pause at bottom] 4x5, Barbell Hip Thrusts 4x6)
* Friday: Accessory Strength Upper (Barbell Close Grip Bench Press 4x6, Heavy Barbell Shrugs 4x8)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 9 Months
Focus: Eccentric Overload & Pin Work.
* Monday: Pin Squats (Barbell Squats starting from the safety pins at parallel 5x4, Eccentric RDLs [4 sec down] 4x6)
* Tuesday: Pin Presses (Barbell Bench Press from pins off chest 5x4, Overhead Pin Press 4x5)
* Wednesday: Eccentric Pulls (Barbell Deadlifts focusing on slow 4-second descent 4x4, Strict Barbell Rows 4x6)
* Thursday: Core & Grip Strength (Barbell Suitcase Deadlifts 4x5/side, Heavy Barbell Holds 4x20 seconds)
* Friday: Overload Day (Heavy Barbell Walkouts [unracking 110% of 1RM and holding for 10s] 4 sets, Heavy Front Squats 4x4)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 12 Months
Focus: Peaking & Cluster Sets (Lifting near absolute max capacity).
* Monday: Squat Clusters (Barbell Back Squat: 4 sets of [1 rep at 90%, rack for 15s, 1 rep, rack for 15s, 1 rep])
* Tuesday: Bench Clusters (Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets of [1 rep at 90%, rack for 15s, 1 rep, rack for 15s, 1 rep])
* Wednesday: Deadlift Clusters (Barbell Deadlift: 4 sets of [1 rep at 90%, reset for 15s, 1 rep, reset for 15s, 1 rep])
* Thursday: Weak Point Training (Barbell Spoto Press 4x5, Barbell Box Squats 4x5)
* Friday: The "Big 3" Primer (Work up to a heavy single [RPE 8-9] in the Squat, Bench, and Deadlift)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day
