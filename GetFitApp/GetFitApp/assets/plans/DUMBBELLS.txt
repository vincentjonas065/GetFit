========================================
TRAINING PLANS: DUMBBELLS
========================================

----------------------------------------
GOAL: LOSE WEIGHT
----------------------------------------

Commitment: 1 Month
**ONE MONTH? STOP PLAYING.**

Commitment: 3 Months
Focus: Foundation Fat Loss (High volume, short rest intervals of 45 seconds).
* Monday: Full Body (DB Goblet Squats 3x15, DB Bent-Over Rows 3x15, DB Floor Press 3x15)
* Tuesday: Upper Body & Core (DB Shoulder Press 3x15, DB Bicep Curls 3x15, DB Weighted Crunches 3x20)
* Wednesday: Lower Body (DB Reverse Lunges 3x12/leg, DB Romanian Deadlifts 3x15, DB Calf Raises 3x20)
* Thursday: DB HIIT Circuits (30 sec work / 30 sec rest: DB Thrusters, DB Renegade Rows, DB Swings - 5 rounds)
* Friday: Full Body (DB Step-ups 3x12/leg, DB Push-ups 3x15, DB Pullovers 3x15)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 6 Months
Focus: Advanced Metabolic Conditioning. Progresses from the 3-month plan by reducing rest to 30 seconds and adding DB Complexes.
* Monday: DB Complex 1 (5 rounds unbroken: 10 DB Cleans, 10 DB Front Squats, 10 DB Push Presses)
* Tuesday: Upper Body Density (AMRAP in 15 mins: 10 DB Chest Flyes, 10 DB Rows)
* Wednesday: Lower Body (DB Bulgarian Split Squats 4x10/leg, DB Goblet Sumo Squats 4x15)
* Thursday: DB Complex 2 (5 rounds unbroken: 10 DB Snatches, 10 DB Reverse Lunges, 10 DB Burpees)
* Friday: Total Body Burnout (DB Thrusters 4x15, DB Renegade Rows 4x12, DB Swings 4x20)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 9 Months
Focus: Heavy/Light Contrast. alternating heavy days for muscle retention with light days for maximum calorie burn.
* Monday: Heavy Full Body (DB Front Squats 4x8, DB Flat Press 4x8, Rest 90s)
* Tuesday: Light Metcon (DB Thrusters 5x20, DB Swings 5x20, Rest 30s)
* Wednesday: Heavy Lower Body (DB RDLs 4x8, DB Walking Lunges 4x10/leg, Rest 90s)
* Thursday: Light Upper/Core (DB Shadow Boxing 5x1 min, DB Russian Twists 4x30, Rest 30s)
* Friday: DB Gasser Circuit (10-to-1 descending ladder: DB Devil Presses, DB Goblet Squats)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 12 Months
Focus: The Ultimate Shred. A periodized block alternating 4 weeks of heavy lifting with 4 weeks of extreme endurance.
* Monday: Push Focus (DB Arnold Press 4x12, DB Floor Flyes 4x15, DB Tricep Extensions 4x15)
* Tuesday: Pull Focus (DB Single-Arm Rows 4x12/arm, DB Pullovers 4x15, DB Hammer Curls 4x15)
* Wednesday: Leg Focus (DB Front Squats 5x15, DB RDLs 5x15, DB Calf Raises 5x20)
* Thursday: DB EMOM (Every Minute on the Minute for 20 mins: 12 DB Thrusters)
* Friday: Full Body Metcon (DB Devil Presses 4x12, DB Farmer’s Carries 4x45 sec)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

GOAL: BUILD ENDURANCE
----------------------------------------

Commitment: 1 Month
**ONE MONTH? STOP PLAYING.**

Commitment: 3 Months
Focus: Muscular Stamina (15-20 rep ranges, 30-45 seconds rest).
* Monday: Full Body Stamina (DB Goblet Squats 3x20, DB Push-Ups 3xMax, DB Rows 3x20)
* Tuesday: Core & Carry (DB Farmer's Walk 4x60 sec, DB Russian Twists 3x40)
* Wednesday: Lower Body Stamina (DB Walking Lunges 3x20/leg, DB Glute Bridges 3x25)
* Thursday: Upper Body Stamina (DB Shoulder Press 3x20, DB Lateral Raises 3x20, DB Curls 3x20)
* Friday: Total Body Flow (Light DB Swings 4x25, DB Thrusters 3x20)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 6 Months
Focus: Time Under Tension (Timed sets rather than rep counting).
* Monday: Lower Body (40 sec work/20 sec rest: DB Squats, DB Lunges, DB RDLs - 4 rounds)
* Tuesday: Upper Push (40 sec work/20 sec rest: DB Floor Press, DB Flyes, DB Overhead Press - 4 rounds)
* Wednesday: Upper Pull (40 sec work/20 sec rest: DB Rows, DB Pullovers, DB Curls - 4 rounds)
* Thursday: Core Endurance (45 sec work/15 sec rest: DB Weighted Plank, DB Sit-ups - 4 rounds)
* Friday: Full Body Marathon (50 sec work/10 sec rest: DB Thrusters, DB Swings, DB Step-ups - 4 rounds)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 9 Months
Focus: Density Training (Perform as many reps as possible in a set time block).
* Monday: Block 1 - 15 Mins (DB Goblet Squats + DB Push-Ups)
* Tuesday: Block 2 - 15 Mins (DB RDLs + DB Rows)
* Wednesday: Block 3 - 15 Mins (DB Lunges + DB Shoulder Press)
* Thursday: Block 4 - 15 Mins (DB Swings + DB Weighted Crunches)
* Friday: Block 5 - 20 Mins Total Body (DB Thrusters + DB Renegade Rows)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day

Commitment: 12 Months
Focus: Extreme Stamina & High-Rep Complexes.
* Monday: The 100-Rep Challenge (Break into sets as needed: 100 DB Squats, 100 DB Rows)
* Tuesday: The 100-Rep Challenge (100 DB Floor Presses, 100 DB Lunges)
* Wednesday: Active Recovery Flow (Light DB Turkish Get-Ups 10 mins continuous)
* Thursday: DB Iron Marathon (Continuous DB Complex for 20 minutes without dropping weight)
* Friday: The 100-Rep Challenge (100 DB Thrusters, 100 DB Swings)
* Saturday: Off / Rest Day
* Sunday: Off / Rest Day
